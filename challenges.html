Challenges and Limitations

Pose-estimation accuracy can depend strongly on the visual conditions under which a person is observed. Even a capable model can produce less reliable landmark estimates when the scene is difficult to interpret.

Listen to Page Overview

Occlusion

Body landmarks may become difficult to estimate when a limb is hidden behind another body part, object, or person.

Camera Angle

A movement viewed from different angles can change how joint positions appear in the 2D image and can affect measurements derived from those coordinates.

Lighting and Background

Poor lighting, low contrast, or visually complex backgrounds can make reliable visual detection more difficult.

Fast Movement

Rapid movement can introduce motion blur and make landmark positions less stable between video frames.

Exercise and Body Position

Unusual poses, self-occlusion, or positions that differ substantially from examples represented during model development can make pose estimation more difficult.
